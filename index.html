Abstract

Action-supervised fine-tuning of vision-language-action (VLA) policies can compress visual structure that is weakly constrained by the action objective. We formalize this as residual visual collapse along local action fibers.

FiberTune is a training-time objective that preserves teacher-structured visual residuals without adding inference-time overhead. It uses an online action probe to estimate action-predictive feature directions, filters them from intermediate token representations, and preserves the resulting residuals through centered directional alignment with a frozen visual teacher and effective-rank regularization.

Under matched training conditions, FiberTune improves over task-loss-only fine-tuning in all six simulation settings spanning CALVIN and LIBERO with pi0.5 and OpenVLA-OFT, and improves physical SO-101 pick-and-place success. Representative gains include CALVIN ABC-to-D SR(5) increasing from 61.4% to 72.1% and SO-101 success from 72.7% to 78.1%. Representation analyses support the preservation of visual information in the residuals.

Method

Residual preservation after action-probe filtering

What FiberTune changes

FiberTune preserves visual structure in a training-only residual branch alongside the policy's action-prediction path.

  • A shared online action probe estimates action-predictive directions; a complementary filter constructs the visual residual.
  • Teacher alignment compares centered, normalized token directions from the residual and frozen visual teacher.
  • Effective-rank prior discourages low-rank residual collapse.
  • Teacher, probe, and adapter are removed after fine-tuning.

Physical Robot Evaluation

SO-101 pick-and-place

We evaluate FiberTune on an SO-101 robot using the instruction "pick up the {color} block and put it into the black box". Yellow, orange, and purple are in-distribution colors; green is held out from training.

Diagnostics

Preserved residual visual structure

FiberTune increases teacher CKA and effective rank in the probe-filtered residual across all six evaluated settings.

Controlled color interventions and semantic readouts

Changing block color while holding the command, robot state, geometry, and optimal-action label fixed reveals amplified visual responses in the probe-filtered residual.

A dimension-matched readout of five object-location and slot-occupancy targets achieves 77.09% macro balanced accuracy in the FiberTune residual, compared with 51.16% in its probe-predictive component, using scene-split cross-validation. See Appendix C.8 for details.
